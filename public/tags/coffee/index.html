Best Cafes in Knoxville

During this time, I have explored a fair bit of cafes around. Not all cafes, but most cafes within 5 mile radius of the university. That covers almost every cafe in downtown Knoxville, the university area, and South Knoxville. I’ve had many friends and colleagues ask me about my favorite spots for coffee, so I thought it would be helpful to share my experiences.
