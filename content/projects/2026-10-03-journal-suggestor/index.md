Choosing a journal starts with a practical question: where are people publishing work related to mine? Journal Suggestor takes a manuscript title and abstract and uses public scholarly metadata from OpenAlex to identify journals publishing related research.

Each suggestion includes the articles behind the match. You can inspect journal profiles, build a shortlist, compare journals, and export records as PDF or CSV.

The project accompanies our working paper, *How to identify an appropriate journal for your manuscript: A predictive modeling approach*, with Pritam Ranjan and Reshu Agarwal.
