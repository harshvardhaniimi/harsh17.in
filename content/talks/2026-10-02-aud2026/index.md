What do humans do when intelligence becomes cheap and available everywhere?
That question guided my talk at *The Future of Humanities in the UAE* at American University in Dubai on October 2, 2026.

I discuss the growing capabilities and falling costs of AI, then turn to the distinction between intelligence and agency: what changes when a system can act on a goal?
The talk makes a case for consilience, bringing technical precision and humanistic judgment into the same decision.
As more tasks become possible to delegate, deciding what we value remains our responsibility.
